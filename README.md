# pool-shot-counter

A simple timer for playing pool. It counts up from 0, turns red and plays an alert sound at 10 seconds, and resets with a button press, including the play/pause button on your earbuds.

## Features
- Counts up with tenths of a second
- Screen turns red and a sound plays at the time limit (default 10 s, adjustable)
- Custom alert sound: choose any audio file, saved in the browser
- Reset by tapping the screen, pressing Space, or pressing your earbud button
- Pause and resume (button or P key)
- Keeps the screen awake while running

## Use
1. Open the page: https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/YOUR-REPO/
2. Connect your earbuds, then tap **Start**
3. Press your earbud button after each shot to reset the clock

## Notes
- Keep the browser open and the screen on, or the earbud button may stop working
- Earbud presses (single, double, triple) all reset the clock
- Pause other music or video apps so they don't take over the media controls

## Hosting
Single file, no build step. Enable GitHub Pages (Settings, Pages, deploy from the main branch).
